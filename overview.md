# SalimSpend

## Project Purpose

SalimSpend is a free personal expense tracking and budgeting application.

The project is designed to help users record their income and expenses, organize transactions into categories, monitor their spending, and manage personal budgets.

The application should provide a simple and accessible experience that helps users understand where their money is going without requiring advanced financial or accounting knowledge.

## Product Vision

SalimSpend aims to make personal money tracking simple, clear, and accessible.

The application should help users quickly understand their income, expenses, spending habits, and budgets without making personal finance feel complicated or overwhelming.

The product should prioritize clarity, simplicity, and ease of use while providing enough information for users to make better-informed decisions about their own spending.

## Core Idea

SalimSpend is built around a simple personal finance workflow:

**Record → Organize → Understand → Manage**

Users record their income and expenses, organize transactions using categories, review their financial activity through summaries and reports, and manage their spending through budgets.

The product should keep this workflow simple and understandable so users can focus on their finances rather than learning a complicated financial system.

## Development Philosophy

SalimSpend will be developed using an AI-assisted software engineering workflow.

The development process should prioritize understanding the problem, defining requirements, designing the solution, and reviewing decisions before implementation.

AI should assist with implementation and problem-solving, but important product and engineering decisions should remain human-reviewed.

The project should favor:

- Clear requirements before coding.
- Small, well-defined tasks.
- Deliberate decomposition of features and tasks.
- Simple and maintainable solutions.
- Verification of the actual application.
- Human review of important changes.
- Iterative improvement based on feedback and verification.
- Clear documentation of important decisions and changes.

The project should avoid unnecessary complexity, premature implementation, and uncontrolled scope growth.

## Important Constraints & Principles

The following constraints and principles should guide the development of SalimSpend.

### Product Constraints

- SalimSpend should be free to use.
- The project should not include payment, subscription, billing, or premium account features for now.
- The product should prioritize simplicity and accessibility.
- Features should provide clear value to the core personal finance experience.
- Unnecessary complexity and scope creep should be avoided.

### Data & Privacy

- Users must only be able to access and manage their own financial data.
- Personal financial information should be handled with appropriate security and access controls.

### Development Principles

- Product and engineering decisions should be human-reviewed.
- AI should assist with implementation, analysis, and problem-solving rather than independently making important product decisions.
- Work should be decomposed into small, well-defined, verifiable tasks.
- The actual application should be verified after meaningful changes.
- Meaningful completed features or milestones should be committed and pushed to GitHub.
- Documentation should be kept current as the project evolves.
- Whenever an AI agent creates or updates a Markdown documentation file, it must explicitly inform the user what file was changed, what was changed, why it was changed, and any relevant impact before the change is considered accepted.
- Documentation changes should be reviewed and approved by the human before they are committed.

These constraints and principles should remain applicable throughout the project unless they are intentionally changed through a human-reviewed decision.
